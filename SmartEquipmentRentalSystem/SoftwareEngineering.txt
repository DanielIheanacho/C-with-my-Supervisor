Drills

For equipments we should be able to

Every piece of equipment has:

A unique identification number.
A name.
A category.
A daily rental price.
A current condition.
A status.

Equipment can be in different states, such as:

Available → Rented → Available

or

Available → Maintenance → Available

You decide exactly how you want to model these states

And the system should be able to:
    Register equipment
    Display Available equipment
    Display equipment currently rented out
    Allow a customer  equipment ---0
    prevent renting an equipment already being rented---0
    Return rented equipment---0
    Display rental history of a particular piece of equipment


Allow equipment to be marked as unavailable when it requires maintenance
Prevent unavailable equipment from being rented
Allow equipment to be reruned to service after maintenance


A customer has information such as:

Identification number
Name
Phone number
Address

For customer we ahould be able to 

Register customers.
Allow a customer to rent equipment. ---0
Prevent a customer from renting equipment that is already rented.---0
Return rented equipment.---0
Calculate the rental cost.
Identify overdue equipment.
Calculate an additional charge when equipment is returned late.
Display a customer's current rentals.
Display the rental history of a customer.

 customer can have multiple rentals.

However, the company doesn't want customers accumulating an unreasonable number of active rentals.

A rental represents the relationship between:

Customer + Equipment + Rental period

For example:

Daniel rents Generator G001 for 5 days.

The rental should keep enough information to determine:

When the rental started.
When it should end.
Whether it has been returned.
How much it should cost.
Whether it is overdue.
Whether an additional charge is required.
Different equipment behaves differently


Not every piece of equipment should necessarily be charged in exactly the same way.

For example:

Standard equipment

Normal calculation:

Daily price × number of days

Heavy equipment

Heavy equipment could have an additional fixed service charge.

Measuring equipment

Measuring instruments might have a calibration requirement.

For example, the company may require a measuring instrument to be inspected after a certain number of rentals.

You should decide how your design represents these differences.

Maintenance

Equipment sometimes develops problems.

For example:

Generator G001 is returned with a damaged cable.

The employee can place the generator into maintenance.

While equipment is under maintenance:

It cannot be rented.
It should not appear among available equipment.
Someone should be able to record why it was sent for maintenance.
It should eventually be returned to service.

You decide how maintenance records should work.
